Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 2pm ship the next business day.

## Tracking your order
Once your order ships you will receive a tracking number by email. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## International shipping
We ship to Taiwan, Japan, and Singapore. International orders take 7-14 business days and may incur customs fees.
